Engagement

How an engagement works

  1. Initial meeting

    At the property or in London. We discuss what you need, and I confirm whether, and how, I can help.

  2. The first hundred days

    A review of the property and its running, introductions to those who work on it, immediate issues resolved and a plan agreed. Charged as a single fixed fee, agreed in advance.

  3. Monthly retainer

    An agreed number of days on site each month, with availability by telephone throughout.
